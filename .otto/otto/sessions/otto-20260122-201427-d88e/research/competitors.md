## Competitive Research

| Product | Key Features | User Praise | User Complaints |
|---------|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| hn-tldr.com | AI summaries, discussion synthesis, thematic breakdowns | Quick to consume, good LLM-powered summaries | No article screenshots, text-heavy |
| tldrhn.io | Concise summaries, sentiment indicators, trending ranking | Clean interface, sentiment analysis | No visual preview of articles |
| hackernews.betacat.io | Multi-language, RSS feed, daily archives | Good filtering, mobile-friendly | Thumbnails but no actual screenshots |
| hckrnews.com | Clean minimalist UI, time-sorted, filter by top % | Fast, responsive design | No summaries, just links |
| Octal/HACK apps | Native mobile experience, collapsible comments | Smooth UX | Navigation bugs, accidental collapses, reset issues |

### Common Pain Points
1. **Text overload** - All-text layouts cause eyestrain when browsing many stories
2. **No visual preview** - Users can't visually scan content before clicking
3. **Navigation friction** - Losing position, accidental collapses, slow loading
4. **Missing login/interaction** - Most alternatives are read-only
5. **Mobile issues** - Poor responsiveness, rotation bugs

### Differentiation Opportunity
Our product differentiates by:
1. **Visual-first approach** - Article screenshots let users visually scan before reading
2. **Complete context in one view** - Screenshot + headline + summary + top comment
3. **Web scraping for actual content** - Not just HN data but fetched article content
4. **LLM summaries of article content** - Not just discussion summaries
5. **Reduced cognitive load** - Visual scanning is faster than reading every headline
